## Technical Details
- **Debugging discipline paid off three times.** #123's issue said "degenerate mesh, fix QTO routing"; per-element extraction showed `volume_mesh_m3 ≈ 4.6e-9`, `surface_count=2`, mesh = 4 side walls + no caps — an open tube.
